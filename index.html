Getting started

WiseOrator uses your device's camera and microphone to give you live feedback during practice sessions — on your posture, facial expressions, and how you speak. Grant camera, microphone, and speech recognition access when prompted so the app can analyze your session.

Apple Intelligence

WiseOrator uses Apple Intelligence to generate personalized session feedback on-device. This feature is currently available in the U.S. region, on supported devices with Apple Intelligence enabled.

Resizable window

WiseOrator's interface is fully resizable, adapting to any window size on iPad, including Split View and Stage Manager.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the app need camera and microphone access?
WiseOrator analyzes your posture and expressions through the camera, and your speech through the microphone, entirely to generate your session feedback. Recordings and analysis stay on your device.

Why is speech feedback not appearing?
Make sure Speech Recognition access is enabled in Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition, and that you're speaking in a supported language.

Why don't I see AI-generated feedback?
AI-generated session feedback requires Apple Intelligence, which is currently available in the U.S. region on supported devices. Make sure Apple Intelligence is enabled in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri.

How do I delete a session?
Open the session from your session history and use the delete option to remove it, including its recording, from your device.
