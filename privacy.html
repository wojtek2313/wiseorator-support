Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Overview

WiseOrator is designed to process everything on your device. We do not operate a backend server, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data to anyone.

Information we use

To provide live coaching feedback, WiseOrator accesses:

  • Camera — to analyze your posture and facial expressions during a session.
  • Microphone — to analyze your speaking pace and detect filler words.
  • Speech recognition — to transcribe your speech for analysis.

All analysis happens locally, on your device. Session recordings and results are stored locally in the app's storage and are never uploaded to us or any third party.

Apple Intelligence

WiseOrator uses Apple Intelligence, on supported devices with Apple Intelligence enabled, to generate personalized session feedback on-device. This feature is currently available in the U.S. region. Processing is handled by Apple's on-device system model, governed by Apple's own privacy practices.

Data storage and deletion

Session data (metrics, recordings, and feedback) is stored only on your device. You can delete any session, including its recording, at any time from your session history. Deleting a session removes it permanently from your device.

Third parties

WiseOrator does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not share your data with anyone, because we never collect it in the first place.

Children's privacy

WiseOrator is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, the app's release notes.
